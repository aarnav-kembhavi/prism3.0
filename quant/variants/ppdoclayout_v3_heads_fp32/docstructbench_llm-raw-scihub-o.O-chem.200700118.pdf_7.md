The matrix photochemistry of methyl thiofluoroformate,
FC(O)SCH3, follows closely the precedents set by the corresponding thiochloroformate, ClC(O)SCH3.[9] The various reaction channels have been charted by characterization of
the photoproducts by their IR spectra and by monitoring
the behavior of the IR absorptions as a function of photolysis time. The conclusions have been endorsed by studying
the effects of perdeuteration of the parent compound and
carrying out quantum chemical calculations on the putative
